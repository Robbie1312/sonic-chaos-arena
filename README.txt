==============================================================
                   SONIC CHAOS ARENA  -  DEMO
          A 3v3 Sonic tag-team fighting fan game
                     by Robbie Melville
==============================================================

Sonic Chaos Arena is a non-profit fan game, made by a fan, for
fans. It is free, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed or
sponsored by SEGA.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW TO START THE GAME
--------------------------------------------------------------

First, unzip the whole folder. The game needs every file in it
sitting next to it, so don't move sonic-chaos-arena.html out on
its own.

 WINDOWS
   Double-click  "Play Sonic Chaos Arena (Windows).bat"
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
   "More info" and then "Run anyway".
   (Or just open sonic-chaos-arena.html in Chrome or Edge.)

 MAC
   Right-click  "Play Sonic Chaos Arena (Mac).command"  and
   choose Open. The first time, macOS asks because the file
   was downloaded - click Open again.
   (Or just open sonic-chaos-arena.html in Chrome.)

 Google Chrome is recommended. Keep the small launcher window
 open while you play, and close it when you're done.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 GAME MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------

 ARCADE ........ Pick a team and fight four CPU teams in a row,
                 each tougher than the last.
 VERSUS CPU .... One battle. Choose the CPU's team yourself, or
                 let RANDOM TEAM pick it.
 VERSUS 2P ..... Two players on one screen.
 TRAINING ...... Practise on a dummy with infinite meter. The
                 dummy can block, jump, super dash, clash heavies
                 or fight back as a CPU.
 HOW TO PLAY ... An interactive tutorial with Knuckles on Angel
                 Island, plus the controls at a glance.
 OPTIONS ....... Volume, rival music, announcer voice, screen
                 shake, time limit, team size and more.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------

                     PLAYER 1        PLAYER 2        GAMEPAD
 Move / jump         W A S D         Arrow keys      Left stick
 Light attack        J               Numpad 4        X
 Medium attack       K               Numpad 5        Y
 Heavy attack        L               Numpad 6        B
 Blast / specials    I               Numpad 8        A
 Super dash          Space           Numpad 0        RT
 Super (1 bar)       H               Numpad 1        LT
 Level 3 super       Down + H        Down + Num 1    Down + LT
 Vanish (1 bar)      ;  or  K + L    Numpad 2        Y + B
 Grab                J + K           Num 4 + Num 5   X + Y
 Assists             U / O           Numpad 7 / 9    LB / RB
 Tag in              Hold U / O      Hold Num 7 / 9  Hold LB / RB
 Surge (once)        U + O           Num 7 + Num 9   LB + RB
 Pause               Esc             Numpad Enter    Start

 Specials: I on its own, Forward + I, and Down + I.
 Hold AWAY from your opponent to block (crouch to block lows).
 Mash L L L L for an auto combo. Light > Medium > Heavy chain
 into each other when they hit, and Down + Heavy launches.
